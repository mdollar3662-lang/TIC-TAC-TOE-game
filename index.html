How to Play

1

Choose a game mode — play against the SahyoNix AI, or challenge a friend locally.

2

Pick your symbol and, against the AI, choose a difficulty: Easy, Medium, or Hard.

3

Take turns placing your X or O on the board — one tap per cell.

4

Get three of your symbol in a row — across, down, or diagonally — to win.

5

Watch your opponent's line-ups and block them before they complete three in a row.

6

If all nine cells fill up with no winner, the round ends in a draw.
